Difficulty Level: Fortune 100 Scale

Business Scenario

A global automotive manufacturer has acquired six electric vehicle (EV) companies across North America, Europe, Japan, South Korea, Australia, and Brazil over the past three years.

Each acquired company operates independently and uses different business applications for sales, customer service, manufacturing, connected vehicles, warranties, roadside assistance, subscriptions, and dealer operations.

The board has announced a new initiative:

"One Customer. One Vehicle. One Experience."

Within 18 months, every customer should have a seamless experience regardless of which brand they purchased from.

Current Landscape

The organisation consists of:

6 acquired companies
14 vehicle brands
Operations in 47 countries
92,000 employees
11,500 dealerships
68 million registered customers
125 million connected vehicles
9 regional contact centres
28 manufacturing plants
17 regional distribution centres
Existing Technology Landscape

The enterprise currently operates:

Multiple CRM platforms inherited through acquisitions
Four independent ERP ecosystems
Three customer identity providers
Separate warranty platforms
Independent dealer management systems
Different mobile applications for each brand
Multiple regional data warehouses
Independent customer loyalty programmes
Different analytics platforms

No system shares a globally trusted customer identifier.

Business Challenges

The executive leadership team has identified several strategic problems:

Customers own vehicles from multiple brands but are treated as different people in every business unit.
Dealers cannot view a customer's history if the customer purchased a vehicle from another brand.
Roadside assistance teams lack visibility into vehicles manufactured by acquired companies.
Subscription services operate independently, forcing customers to manage multiple accounts.
Vehicle software updates are coordinated differently by each company.
Customer support quality varies significantly across regions.
Executives cannot obtain a global view of customer lifetime value.
Global recalls require manual coordination across multiple organisations.
Marketing campaigns frequently target the same customer multiple times because duplicate customer records exist across brands.
Enterprise Constraints

The board has imposed the following constraints:

The business cannot suspend operations during the transformation.
Each acquired company must continue operating independently for legal reasons during the transition period.
Several countries have strict data residency requirements preventing unrestricted movement of customer data.
Existing dealer integrations cannot be modified for at least two years due to contractual commitments.
Annual technology spending has been reduced by 25%.
The CEO expects measurable business value within the first nine months.
The company plans to acquire two additional EV manufacturers next year, and the future architecture must accommodate further acquisitions with minimal disruption.

## Question 9: What are the enterprise use cases for Agentforce?

### Answer

Agentforce is designed for enterprise-grade AI automation across a wide range of business functions. Its capabilities in reasoning, action execution, grounding, and security make it suitable for complex, high-stakes scenarios where traditional chatbots and rule-based automation fall short.

### Architecture Explanation

**Customer Service**
- Automated ticket resolution for common issues
- Personalized product recommendations
- Order tracking and status updates
- Returns and refunds processing
- Account management and profile updates
- Escalation to human agents when needed

**Sales**
- Lead qualification and scoring
- Personalized sales outreach
- Product recommendation based on customer data
- Quote generation and proposal creation
- Follow-up automation
- CRM data enrichment

**Marketing**
- Personalized campaign content generation
- Lead nurturing and engagement
- Event registration and management
- Survey distribution and analysis
- Social media engagement
- Content personalization

**Finance**
- Account balance inquiries

**Healthcare**

### Real-World Example

A multinational insurance company deploys Agentforce across three business lines:
1. **Customer Service**: Automated claims filing with status tracking, reducing call volume by 40%
2. **Sales**: Lead qualification agent that scores prospects and schedules appointments, increasing conversion by 25%
3. **Field Service**: Technician dispatch agent that creates work orders, checks parts availability, and schedules visits, reducing dispatch time by 60%

Each agent is configured with domain-specific Topics, Actions, and Prompt Templates, with shared grounding in the company's Data Cloud and CRM data.

### Common Mistakes

- Trying to use Agentforce for every use case without evaluating fit
- Not scoping the use case appropriately (too broad or too narrow)
- Ignoring the need for human escalation paths
- Not considering the data requirements for grounding

### Interview Tips

- Tailor use cases to the interviewer's company or industry
- Emphasize the business value, not just the technical capabilities
- Mention specific metrics (cost reduction, efficiency gains, customer satisfaction)
- Show awareness of limitations and when human intervention is needed
